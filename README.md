Solmara is an original sun-soaked coastal open-world crime game. Explore the city of Solmara on foot and by car: steal cars, weave through traffic and pedestrians, and outrun the police as your wanted level rises. Take on five story missions, fight your way through trouble, and track it all on a HUD with a minimap. Your cash and mission progress save between visits, so the city remembers what you did.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Keyboard and mouse: move with WASD or the arrow keys, enter and steal cars with E, attack with the mouse, pause with P, and mute with M.
